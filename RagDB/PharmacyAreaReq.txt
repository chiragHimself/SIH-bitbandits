Guidelines for Pharmacy Degree Institutions (Room and Space Requirements)
Room Dimensions and Carpet Area:

Class Rooms: 66 sqm per room.
Tutorial Rooms: 33 sqm per room.
Laboratories (Includes Machine Room & Instrumentation Room): 75 sqm per room.
Research Laboratories: 75 sqm per room.
Animal House: 75 sqm.
Computer Center: 75 sqm.
Library & Reading Room: 150 sqm.
Seminar Hall: 132 sqm.
Number of Rooms Required (For New Institutions):

Class Rooms: Equal to A (number of divisions per class of 18 students).
Tutorial Rooms: Calculated as C = A.
Laboratories: Calculated as D = C/4 (rounded to the nearest integer).
Laboratories (Includes Machine Room & Instrumentation Room): 4 per course.
Animal House, Computer Center, Library & Reading Room, and Seminar Hall: 1 each.
Total Number of Rooms (UG):

Class Rooms: Equal to A.
Tutorial Rooms: Equal to C = A × 4.
Laboratories: Calculated as D = C/4 × 12.
Other facilities (Animal House, Computer Center, Library & Reading Room, Seminar Hall): 1 each.
Total Number of Rooms (PG):

Total rooms: F = 2 × H, where H = 1 per specialization.
Laboratories: 1 per specialization.
Key Notes:

Laboratories include Machine Room & Instrumentation Room.
Classrooms, Tutorial Rooms, and Laboratories required for 2nd, 3rd, and 4th-year programs may be added progressively (calculated as 3+3+2).
UG laboratories shared for PG courses must be upgraded to meet PG curriculum requirements.
Fractions in calculations must be rounded to the next integer.
