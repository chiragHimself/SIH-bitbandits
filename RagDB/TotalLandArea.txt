Land Area Requirements for AICTE Approval
Location Classification:

Institutions are classified based on their location:
Rural Places: Defined by the competent authority as rural.
Other than Rural Places: Certified by the competent authority as not located in a rural area.
Land Area Requirements for Technical Programs:

Engineering & Technology:
In rural places: Minimum land required is 10 acres for UG programs, 5 acres for diploma programs, and 10 acres for PG programs.
In non-rural places: Minimum land required is 2.5 acres for UG programs, 1.5 acres for diploma programs, and 2.5 acres for PG programs.
Pharmacy:
In rural places: Minimum land required is 2 acres for all programs (UG, diploma, and PG).
In non-rural places: Minimum land required is 0.75 acres for all programs (UG, diploma, and PG).
Architecture & Town Planning:
In rural places: Minimum land required is 2.5 acres for all programs (UG, diploma, and PG).
In non-rural places: Minimum land required is 1 acre for all programs (UG, diploma, and PG).
Applied Arts & Crafts:
In rural places: Minimum land required is 2 acres for all programs (UG, diploma, and PG).
In non-rural places: Minimum land required is 0.75 acres for all programs (UG, diploma, and PG).
Hotel Management & Catering Technology (HMCT):
In rural places: Minimum land required is 2.5 acres for all programs (UG, diploma, and PG).
In non-rural places: Minimum land required is 1 acre for all programs (UG, diploma, and PG).
MCA:
In rural places: Minimum land required is 1.5 acres for PG programs.
In non-rural places: Minimum land required is 0.5 acres for PG programs.
Management:
In rural places: Minimum land required is 1 acre for PG programs.
In non-rural places: Minimum land required is 0.5 acres for PG programs.
Approval Criteria:

The land area must meet the minimum size requirements based on program type and location.
Institutions must obtain certification of location classification (rural or non-rural) from competent authorities.
For institutions offering multiple programs, the land area should be sufficient to cumulatively meet the requirements for all programs offered.
Special Notes:

Rural institutions generally require more land due to infrastructural and operational demands.
Stand-alone post-graduate programs have the same land requirements as undergraduate programs in most cases.
