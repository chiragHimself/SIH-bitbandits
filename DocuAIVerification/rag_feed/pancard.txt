Rules for Verifying a PAN Card (India)
The PAN card has unique identifiers and a specific format under the rules of the Income Tax Department in India. Here’s how the model should validate PAN cards:

Card Format and Structure:

The PAN number should have a 10-character alphanumeric format (e.g., ABCDE1234F), where:
The first five characters are uppercase letters.
The next four characters are digits.
The last character is an uppercase letter.
Check if the PAN number follows the correct sequence and is visible clearly on the card.
Details to Verify:

Name: Verify that the name matches the format, generally in uppercase letters without any special characters.
Father’s Name: The father’s name is typically included. Ensure it appears in uppercase letters and in a reasonable format.
Date of Birth: Should appear in DD-MM-YYYY format. Validate that the date of birth is consistent with the age and matches general expectations (e.g., not a future date).
Document Fields:

PAN Number: This is the most critical part of the PAN card. Ensure the format of the PAN number (5 letters, 4 digits, 1 letter) is correct and follows the rules.
Signature: Check if the signature of the cardholder is present in the designated area, as it is an essential verification element on PAN cards.
Government Emblems and Security Features:

Verify the presence of the Government of India emblem on the PAN card.
Look for additional security features, such as a hologram and watermark, to prevent counterfeits.
Check for signs of tampering or modifications (like blurred or altered text), which can indicate a fake or altered card.
Language:

PAN cards are usually in English, so verify that all text is in English and follows consistent formatting.
Database Cross-Validation (If Available):

Cross-check the PAN details against an official government database to confirm authenticity. (For real-world applications, this may involve accessing the Income Tax Department’s API.)
Additional Unique Identifiers (for New PAN Cards):

Some newer PAN cards may include a QR code. If present, decode and validate the QR code details against the card’s visible information.
